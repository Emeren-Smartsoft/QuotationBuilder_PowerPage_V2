Why EDR, why now

Modern attacks don't drop a file - so signature AV never sees them.

74% of ransomware in 2025 used "living-off-the-land" techniques: PowerShell scripts, in-memory payloads, signed binaries used maliciously. EDR catches the behaviour even when the file is never written to disk.

File-less & in-memory attacks

PowerShell, WMI, signed-binary abuse. No file = nothing for AV to scan. EDR watches the behaviour instead.

Lateral movement & privilege abuse

Once one machine is compromised, attackers pivot using legitimate Windows tools. Only behaviour analytics catches it.

"What just happened?"

Without recorded telemetry, your IT team has no way to investigate scope, blast radius or root cause after an alert.
