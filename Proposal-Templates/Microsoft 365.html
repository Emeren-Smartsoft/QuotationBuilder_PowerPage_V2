Microsoft 365 Plans

Licence summary - pick the right plan per user.

All plans are billed annually per user, exclusive of GST. Mix and match across your organisation - e.g. Business Basic for shop-floor staff, Business Standard for office teams, Business Premium for finance & leadership.

Microsoft 365 Business Basic
Web & mobile Office - cloud essentials
  • Business email (50 GB / user)
  • Microsoft Teams chat & meetings
  • 1 TB OneDrive per user
  • SharePoint intranet & document libraries
  • Web & mobile Word, Excel, PowerPoint
Best for: shop floor, frontline, low-touch office staff.
Microsoft 365 Business Standard
Full desktop apps + cloud collaboration
  • Everything in Business Basic
  • Desktop Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook
  • Install on up to 5 PCs/Macs per user
  • Microsoft Bookings, Clipchamp, Loop
  • Advanced Teams webinars
Best for: sales, accounts, designers, consultants.
Microsoft 365 Business Premium
Productivity + enterprise security
  • Everything in Business Standard
  • Defender for Office 365 - anti-phishing
  • Intune device management & remote-wipe
  • Entra ID P1 - MFA, Conditional Access
  • Information protection & DLP
  • Windows 11 Pro upgrade rights
Best for: finance, leadership, regulated workloads.

  • Calendars, contacts, shared & resource mailboxes
  • Outlook auto-configuration on Windows, Mac, iOS, Android
  • 1 user-training session + 30 days post-cutover support
