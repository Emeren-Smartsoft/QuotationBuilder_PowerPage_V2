Why this portal

Everything your sales team needs

The Quotation portal connects directly to your SharePoint price lists, proposal templates and saved quotations through Power Automate — so the numbers, branding and approvals stay in one governed place.

Live price lists

Categories and products are read straight from the Pricelist library in SharePoint. Update an Excel file and the quotation form picks it up instantly.

Branded proposals

Append any annexure from the Proposal Templates library and export the full quotation as a single, print-ready PDF.

One-click delivery

Save the quotation back to SharePoint and (optionally) email it to the customer via Outlook — all in a single submit.

How it works

From blank page to PDF in three steps

  1. 1

    Choose a category

    The portal pulls the latest folders from Documents/Pricelist/ on SharePoint — no manual sync.

  2. 2

    Pick products & set quantities

    SKUs, titles and prices come directly from the Excel price list. Totals, margins and discounts update live.

  3. 3

    Save, email or download

    Generate the branded PDF, store it back in SharePoint and optionally send it to the customer via Outlook in one click.
